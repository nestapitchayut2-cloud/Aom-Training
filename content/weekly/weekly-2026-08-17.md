### 🔢 โจทย์คำนวณ (5 ข้อ)

1. A nebuliser solution of salbutamol is available as **5 mg/2.5 mL**. What volume, in mL, is needed to deliver a **2.5 mg** dose?

2. A child weighing **18 kg** is prescribed oral prednisolone for an acute asthma exacerbation at a dose of **1 mg/kg** (maximum 40 mg). What dose, in mg, should be given?

3. A patient's peak flow reading is **320 L/min**, and their personal best is **400 L/min**. What percentage of their personal best does this reading represent?

4. A pMDI inhaler contains **200 actuations**. It is prescribed as **2 puffs, twice daily**. How many days will one inhaler last?

5. A patient's inhaled corticosteroid dose is being stepped down from **400 micrograms twice daily**. If the total daily dose is reduced by **25%**, what is the new total daily dose, in micrograms?

*(เฉลยอยู่ท้ายไฟล์)*

### 💊 สรุปคลินิก — Asthma & inhaler technique

**Mechanism (concept level):**
- **Inhaled corticosteroids (ICS)** — "preventer" therapy; reduce airway inflammation over time; must be used regularly, not just when symptomatic, because the anti-inflammatory effect builds up gradually.
- **Short-acting beta-2 agonists (SABA)** — "reliever" therapy; rapid bronchodilation within minutes by relaxing airway smooth muscle; used for acute symptom relief, not as a stand-alone regular treatment.
- **Correct inhaler technique** matters as much as the drug itself — poor technique means the dose never reaches the lower airways.

**Key counselling points (generic, exam-level):**
- A **spacer** improves drug delivery with a pressurised metered-dose inhaler (pMDI) and reduces oropharyngeal side effects (e.g. candidiasis with ICS); dry powder and soft mist inhalers do not need a spacer.
- Rinse the mouth after using an ICS inhaler to reduce the risk of oral thrush.
- Needing a reliever inhaler more than expected, or waking at night with symptoms, are signs that asthma control is worsening and needs review.
- Used inhalers should be returned to a pharmacy for disposal rather than placed in general waste.

### 🗣️ Counselling Card — Asthma & inhaler technique

> **Ness plays:** Priya, 24, recently diagnosed with asthma, collecting her first ICS preventer inhaler (pMDI) and a spacer.
>
> **Opening line:** *"I already have a blue inhaler from when I get out of breath at the gym — do I really need this new one as well?"*
>
> **If asked about symptoms:** *"I've been needing the blue one almost every day this week, actually."*
> **If asked about technique:** admits she "just sprays it into her mouth and breathes in fast" without a spacer.
> **If pushed for detail:** doesn't rinse her mouth after using any inhaler.

**Marking criteria:**
1. Explains clearly why the new inhaler (preventer) is different in purpose from the blue reliever, and why both may be needed
2. **Identifies the frequent reliever use as a sign of poor asthma control** and advises this should be reviewed, not just accepted
3. **Corrects the technique** — demonstrates/explains the role of the spacer and slow, steady inhalation instead of a fast spray
4. Advises rinsing the mouth after the ICS inhaler and explains why (reduces risk of oral thrush)

## 📖 Glossary

**trachea** — หลอดลมใหญ่
**bronchiole** — หลอดลมฝอย
**alveolus** — ถุงลมปอด (พหูพจน์: alveoli)
**hemoglobin** — โปรตีนในเม็ดเลือดแดงที่จับออกซิเจน
**autonomic nervous system** — ระบบประสาทอัตโนมัติ ควบคุมการทำงานของร่างกายโดยไม่รู้ตัว
**intercostal muscles** — กล้ามเนื้อระหว่างซี่โครง
**spacer** — กระบอกต่อยาพ่นเพื่อช่วยให้ยาเข้าปอดได้มากขึ้น
**exacerbation** — อาการกำเริบเฉียบพลัน
**corticosteroid** — สเตียรอยด์ชนิดต้านการอักเสบ
**nebuliser** — เครื่องพ่นยาแบบละอองฝอย
**peak flow meter** — อุปกรณ์วัดอัตราการหายใจออกสูงสุด ใช้ติดตามการทำงานของปอด
**overview** — ย่อหน้าสรุปภาพรวมใน Writing Task 1
